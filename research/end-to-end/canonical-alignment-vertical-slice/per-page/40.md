# 9 The Relativity of Simultaneity

Up to now our considerations have been referred to a particular body of reference, which we have styled a "railway embankment." We suppose a very long train travelling along the rails with the constant velocity v and in the direction indicated in Fig 1. People travelling in this train will with a vantage view the train as a rigid referencebody (co-ordinate system); they regard all events in [Figure: M Train A Embankment B Fig. 1] reference to the train. Then every event which takes place along the line also takes place at a particular point of the train.
